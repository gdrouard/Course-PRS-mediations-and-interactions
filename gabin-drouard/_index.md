\## Bio

Starting October 2026, Gabin Drouard is a University Lecturer of Social Data Science at the faculty of Social Sciences, University of Helsinki. He completed his postdoctoral and doctoral research at the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland (FIMM), University of Helsinki, after training in statistics for life sciences (ENSAI, engineering degree/MSc) and epidemiology (Université Rennes 1, MRes). 



His research sits at the intersection of biomedical informatics and molecular epidemiology, applying so-called omics data (e.g., proteomics, metabolomics) to family-based and twin designs to disentangle the genetic and environmental sources of health variation. His applications include behavioral traits such as weight change and substance use, as well as biological aging, depression and cardiometabolic markers.
